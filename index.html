Add your players, pick a format, and get a fair fixture list with a live leaderboard. Everything is saved in your browser, so you can score as you play.

1. Players

Add at least 3 players for singles, or 4 for doubles.

2. Format

Everyone plays everyone once. Odd numbers get a fair rotation of byes.

3. Play Style

Round robin: everyone plays everyone. Winners stay on: continuous play with rotating losers.
